My Temps Forecasts

How to Play

  • Choose Today or Tomorrow from the forecast day selector.
  • Enter your forecast for the date's High and Low before the cutoff times.
  • Feel free to use NWS, AccuWeather, Weather Channel, WU, LLMs, or current weather to help guide your forecasts.
  • Click Save to save your forecasts. You can edit forecasts until the cutoff times for each city.
  • Click View My Score the next morning to see results verified by NWS and your shareable score. Good luck!
  • Repeat with Hourly Forecasts, then see your results each hour and all of today's hourlies at 8 PM ET. Have fun 😊

Lazy Forecast Penalty

Saving the auto-filled Lazy Forecast temps will apply a –1 Coin and Mood penalty. Also, your streak will not grow. Choose Back to edit any field or choose Save if you accept the penalty for using Lazy Forecast.
